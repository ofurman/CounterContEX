## Notes

- `validity`: fraction of CFs whose discriminator-predicted class differs from factual (higher = better).
- `lof_scores_cf`: mean negative-LOF plausibility score on X_cf (lower = more plausible).
- `sparsity`: mean fraction of feature entries changed (lower = sparser).
- `true_actionability`: fraction of CFs where immutable columns are exactly preserved (must be 1.0 by construction).
- `proximity_l2_jaccard`: mean per-instance L2 distance on *valid* CFs (lower = closer to factual).
- `frac_oob`: fraction of CF rows with at least one feature outside [0,1] before clipping.

## Interpretation

### MOONS — Strong result
- Validity=0.85 exceeds the ≥0.70 target. The 2-D class-separated structure is well-captured.
- LOF=1.055 is excellent: generated CFs are indistinguishable from in-distribution points.
- Sparsity=1.0: all features are actionable (both features changed), expected for 2-feature dataset.
- OOB=0%: no extrapolation artefacts; TabPFN stays within the training range on MOONS.

### HELOC — Validity met but plausibility poor
- Validity=0.66 exceeds the ≥0.50 target. The model successfully conditions on the target class.
- LOF≈2.5B is extremely high: CFs land far outside the training distribution. This is driven by
  the 66% OOB rate — when 17/23 features are masked with only 6 immutable + 1 Y observed,
  TabPFN extrapolates aggressively into feature regions that don't occur in training data.
- After clipping to [0,1], proximity L2=1.652 in scaled space is large (max possible L2
  for a 23-D unit-scaled dataset is √23≈4.8, so 1.65 is substantial change).
- True actionability=1.0: immutable columns exactly preserved by construction — no bugs in
  the column-freezing mechanism.

### Root cause of HELOC OOB / plausibility issues
TabPFN's internal representation uses a bar distribution whose support extends beyond the
observed training range. When conditioning only on 6 immutable features + Y (leaving 17 masked),
the conditioning information is very sparse relative to the number of features. The marginal
density at the imputed values is low. This is exactly the "proximity vs. plausibility" risk
flagged in the plan's risk section. Refinement levers (Stage 6): tighter context selection
(e.g. nearest-neighbor context from target class), lower temperature, or a post-processing
step to project CFs back into the training manifold.
