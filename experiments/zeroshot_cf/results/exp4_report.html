Beam search over TabPFN reaches perfect validity — but only by breaking the constraint

Four cells, two datasets, two regimes. The unconstrained regime flips every test point; the one regime that respects immutable features flips 38%. Every miss was reachable.

Reading the two tables

sparsity uses exact float equality and saturates at 1.0 for continuously-generated counterfactuals — it carries no information here. Use ε-sparsity. The registry's LOF averages over all rows, which is where HELOC frozen's 6.5×10⁶ comes from; the reference convention scores valid rows only and takes a median of logs.

Findings

1
Perfect validity is bought by dropping the constraint

HELOC from-scratch hits validity 1.000 by rewriting essentially the whole row: l0_count_mean 17.96 of 23, true_actionability 0.0, immutable_drift_mean 0.112. It changes all six immutable features, which makes it a sample from the target class rather than an actionable counterfactual.

Law's 1.000 does not test the constraint either — Law has no immutable features, so freeze_immutable masks nothing. Of four cells, exactly one poses the real problem, and it scores 0.376.

2
The 62% of misses were all reachable — this is a search failure, not an infeasible constraint

The discriminator is logistic regression, so the ceiling is closed-form. Pin the 6 immutables at their factual values, let the 17 actionable features range over [0,1], and take the extreme achievable logit by setting each free feature to 1 or 0 according to the sign of its weight: 

target REACHABLE in principle        2092  (1.0000)
target actually reached (validity)    787  (0.3762)
reachable but MISSED                 1305
hard infeasible                         0  (0.0000)

Zero rows are infeasible. The standing hypothesis — that freezing immutables leaves a conditional distribution with no target-class mass — is dead in its geometric form.

Nor are the misses near-misses. Median margin runs from −0.903 at the factual to +20.373 at the best achievable point, and the generated counterfactual travels a median 0.3% of that distance. 496 of the 1305 moved the wrong way, ending further from the target than the factual started. Successes and failures begin from nearly the same place (median factual margin −0.695 vs −0.903), so difficulty does not explain the split.

Caveat

Beam search does not optimise the LR logit — it samples TabPFN's conditional to stay on-manifold, with LR only as the scoring oracle. What this rules out is the infeasibility explanation. What stays open is whether TabPFN's conditional given the pinned features has no target-side mass, or the selector simply is not pushing toward validity. The 496 backwards moves point at the second.

3
The LOF 6.5×10⁶ result was an artifact

Previously treated as the project's central open question — a ~750,000× plausibility gap against Law. Under the reference convention it is 0.0345 against from-scratch's −0.0136. The gap does not exist.

Exactly 115 rows carry LOF > 10³ (max 1.19×10⁸), and they are byte-identical to HELOC's 115-row duplicate block: the all-zeros row, the MinMax image of the −9 "no record" code, which appears 473 times in X_train. A query point sitting on a 473-fold duplicated training point has k-NN distance ≈ 0, so local reachability density diverges and the LOF ratio explodes numerically. Freezing pins those rows there; from-scratch lets them escape.

Frozen validity of 0.376 is untouched by any of this and stands.

4
Relabelling makes one validity definition meaningless

exp4_beam_search.py:144 sets y_target = 1 − disc.predict(X_test). The cel registry defines validity against the true label as mean(y_cf_pred != y_test), which under relabelling reduces algebraically to the discriminator's accuracy:

DatasetDisc. accuracyRegistry validity
heloc0.72320.7232
law0.78150.7815

Identical to four decimals. Use y_cf_pred == y_target. The reference repo's own pipelines target 1 − y_test, where the two definitions coincide — the divergence is specific to this project's protocol.

Numbers that must not be reported as published values

  • Law sparsity_categorical = 1.000. The formula uses exact float equality and assumes discrete one-hots. Beam search emits a continuous relaxation — 0.0% of Law's categorical cells are exactly 0 or 1, and some rows carry two columns near 1 within one group, so the output is not on the simplex. Decoded by argmax, the real change rate is 0.235.
  • pairwise_diversity_mixed is N/A, not 0. It measures spread across the K counterfactuals a method proposes per factual; this method emits one. Run verbatim it returns 0.0 for HELOC only because it groups by factual value and finds the 115-row duplicate block, whose deterministic counterfactuals are identical.
  • ε-sparsity is inflated by construction. The relative denominator |x| + 1e−8 on MinMax-scaled data means a feature at x ≈ 0 makes any change count as significant.

Provenance and open issues

Hardware confound

The two Law regimes were expected to be numerically identical and are not — they agree on validity but differ throughout (LOF 9.83 vs 8.72). law/frozen was generated locally on MPS, law/fromscratch on a GH200 under CUDA, and TabPFN's outputs are not bitwise portable across backends. The difference cannot be attributed to the regime until law/frozen is rerun on the cluster. The HELOC comparison is unaffected — both cells share a backend.

Metric definitions are verbatim ports of cel/metrics/dicoflex_metrics.py at commit b9715ef on origin/ofurman/CFN_baselines, the module wired by dicoflex.yaml and identified as the source of the paper's Table 1. It is absent from that repo's working tree and had to be read out of git history. Three of its formulas differ from the generic registry: relative ε-sparsity, categorical sparsity over one-hot columns rather than groups, and euclidean+hamming diversity.

  • The 115 all-zeros HELOC rows are a missing-data code, not records. They are 5.5% of the test split, they break LOF outright, and they touch every metric computed on HELOC. Decide whether they belong in the evaluation set at all.
  • --chunk-size changes results. TabPFN's predictions depend on batch composition; chunk=40 vs chunk=7 differed by ~1.0 on a one-hot column. Pinned at 4096 so a whole target class is one call. Hold it fixed across compared cells.
  • Cluster runs are rsynced from the working tree, not a git ref. Until the branch is pushed, no cluster result is reproducible from a commit.
